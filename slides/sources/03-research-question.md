## Speaker notes

I am reading this out because every clause was chosen to be falsifiable.

Locally fine-tuned — no API, and training fits on the same card. Both a prompt and a reference
image — two signals that can disagree, which turned out to matter. Multiple visually distinct
styles — distinct is a claim needing evidence, not an adjective. Reproducible quality, and I will
come back to that phrase, because it is where my answer splits in half.

And eight gigabytes. Not "a laptop", not "modest hardware". A number, so a configuration either
fits or it does not.

Underneath sit twelve secondary questions: eight answered within scope, four bounded, one component
