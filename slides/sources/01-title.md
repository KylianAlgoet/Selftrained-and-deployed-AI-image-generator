## Speaker notes

Good morning. I am Kylian Algoet, and this is DeckForge AI.

It lets someone describe a skateboard deck graphic in words, pick a style, optionally give it a
reference image, and see the artwork on a 3D deck before downloading it. The model is fine-tuned
locally, on this laptop, on a dataset I built.

What I am defending is not the demo. The demo takes four minutes and it works. It is the research:
a measured account of what fits in eight gigabytes of video memory, what does not, and how I
established the difference rather than assuming it.

Several of the answers are the opposite of what I expected. Those are the parts I will spend time
on.
