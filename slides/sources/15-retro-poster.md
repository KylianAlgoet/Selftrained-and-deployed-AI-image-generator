## Speaker notes

One of my three styles is a partial pass, and I would rather present it than have you find it.

The training material is WPA silkscreen posters from the Library of Congress — so almost all of it
has a frame, a border and large display lettering as part of the composition.

The adapter learned all of that. Ask for a mountain and you often get a mountain inside a poster
frame with lettering that is not quite text. Not what a decal needs.

Three options: drop the style and lose a third of the deliverable, re-score it until it passed, or
ship it labelled. I shipped it labelled — marked partial in the interface, so the user is told
before choosing it.

I know the likely fix, and it landed after the freeze. Future work, not a claim.
