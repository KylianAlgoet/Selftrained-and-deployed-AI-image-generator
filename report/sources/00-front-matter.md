## How to read this report

Every quantitative claim in this document is traceable to a file in the repository, and the path is
given where the claim is made. Figures cite the evidence directory they were produced in. Experiment
identifiers (`EXP-###`), decision records (`DR-###`), research questions (`RQ##`) and risks (`R##`)
all resolve to real records, and a validation script asserts that they do before this PDF is built.

Numbers that appear in more than one place — test counts, the generation total, memory figures,
checkpoint hashes — are not typed into the text. They are substituted at build time from
`report/facts.yaml`, where each one is proved against the evidence file that states it. A figure
that drifts from its evidence fails the build rather than reaching this page.

**Failed and blocked work is reported as a result, not omitted.** Sections 12 and 18 exist for that
purpose, and the conclusions in section 19 are qualified where the evidence is qualified.

## Use of AI assistance

This project was carried out with Claude Code (Anthropic) as an engineering and documentation
assistant, and that assistance is disclosed rather than implied. A full account — what the assistant
did, what the student decided, and how results were verified independently — is in section 6.4, and
the session-by-session record is in `docs/ai-usage.md`.

Three statements govern the rest:

- **Every research conclusion, rubric score and production selection in this report is the student's.**
  No score was generated by an assistant, and no decision record was concluded without a human gate.
- **No generation was ever run by an assistant.** All {{ facts.generations_total }} GPU generations
  were authorised and executed under the student's control.
- **The AI did not validate its own results.** Measurements come from instrumented runs and tests;
  judgements come from the student.

## Reproducing this document

```
python scripts/build_report.py
```

The build renders the Markdown in `report/sources/`, applies the template in
`report/templates/`, and prints the result to PDF with headless Chrome. Its design, the
alternatives considered, and its stated limitations are recorded in `DR-015`.
